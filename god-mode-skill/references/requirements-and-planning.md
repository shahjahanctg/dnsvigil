# Requirements, Planning, and Traceability

Use `core/requirements-and-planning.md` for the universal planning contract.

## When to create each artifact
- **Brief:** simple work with one clear outcome.
- **Requirements/specification:** multiple stakeholders, features, constraints, or acceptance conditions.
- **Architecture/workflow:** multiple components, integrations, deployment contexts, or significant dependencies.
- **Task/dependency plan:** work spanning multiple steps or contributors.
- **Risk/recovery plan:** consequential, hard-to-reverse, production, regulated, or security-sensitive work.
- **Traceability matrix:** complex, regulated, audited, or multi-milestone work.
- **Decision record:** choices with meaningful trade-offs or future consequences.
- **Completion/convergence report:** substantial work, audits, releases, or user-requested evidence.

## Clarification rule
Resolve blockers before plan approval. Do not require users to answer questions whose answers would not materially change the plan. Disclose assumptions and their impact.

## Cross-domain acceptance criteria
Criteria may be quantitative, qualitative, visual, procedural, evidentiary, or experiential. They should still be observable enough to support an honest completion decision.
