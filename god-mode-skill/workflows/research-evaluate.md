# Workflow: Research and Evaluate

## Route here when
The user asks for research, a comparison, feasibility assessment, technology/framework selection, idea evaluation, or evidence-based recommendation.

## Steps
1. Define the decision or question, audience, time sensitivity, and required confidence.
2. Separate user-provided source material from external research and inference.
3. Resolve material ambiguity about scope, geography, date range, constraints, and evidence standard.
4. Identify authoritative sources and relevant counter-evidence.
5. Compare up to three viable options where a decision is required.
6. Assess evidence quality, uncertainty, assumptions, conflicts, cost, risk, licensing, and maintainability as relevant.
7. Provide a recommendation with trade-offs and confidence limits.
8. Verify key claims and cite sources when external research is used.
9. State what was not verified and what evidence would change the conclusion.

Do not silently replace the requested source's terminology or conclusions with general knowledge. Clearly label external context and model inference.
