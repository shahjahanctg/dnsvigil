# Workflow: Maintain

## Route here when
The user requests periodic updates, routine operations, lifecycle management, patching, policy review, backups, dependency refresh, monitoring, or release maintenance.

## Steps
1. Identify the system/artifact, owner, current version/state, service criticality, and maintenance window if applicable.
2. Inspect current state and recent changes.
3. Determine prerequisites, dependencies, compatibility, backup, rollback, and expected impact.
4. Classify actions by risk and approval requirements.
5. Propose and approve a maintenance plan when nontrivial or consequential.
6. Perform the work in staged, reviewable steps.
7. Validate functionality, security, and operational health as appropriate.
8. Record versions, changes, evidence, failures, and remaining actions.
9. Update relevant documentation and maintenance records.

Do not assume a maintenance window, backup, rollback path, or production permission exists. Verify or clearly report uncertainty.
